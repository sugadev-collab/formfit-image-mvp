Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Your images never leave your device

FormFit Image resizes and compresses images inside your web browser. When you choose a photo or signature, it is read and processed on your own phone or computer. The file is not uploaded to any server, and we never see, store or share your images.

Cookies and accounts

There are no accounts and no login. The site doesn't set its own cookies.

Usage statistics

To learn whether the tool is useful, we may use a privacy-friendly, cookie-free statistics service (planned: GoatCounter). It counts page views and anonymous events such as "image processed" or "download clicked", along with general information such as browser type and country. It never receives your images, file names or personal details.

Hosting

The website is served by a static hosting provider, which may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability.

Advertising

The site currently shows no ads. If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated first.

Not an official government website

FormFit Image is an independent tool. It is not connected to any exam body, government department or recruitment portal. Always check the official notice for your form's requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's public repository.
