How to prepare your SSC upload

  1. Open the official notice for your exact SSC exam and find the photograph and signature section.
  2. Choose Photo or Signature, then type the notice's Min KB, Max KB and, if given, the dimensions.
  3. Choose your image, tap Resize & Compress, check that every item shows ✅, then download the JPG and upload it on the SSC portal.

Photo: some SSC exams ask for a photograph captured live through the application system instead of an uploaded file. If your notice says this, follow the portal's camera instructions. This tool is only for files you need to upload yourself.

Signature: sign with a dark pen on plain white paper, photograph it from directly above in good light, and crop the extra paper. If the notice gives the size in cm, keep the same width-to-height ratio in pixels (for example, 4 × 2 cm has the same ratio as 400 × 200 px).

Frequently asked questions

Do all SSC exams use the same photo and signature size?

No. Requirements can differ between SSC examinations and between years. Always use the values in the official notice for your exact exam.

The notice gives the signature size in cm. What do I enter?

Enter the KB limits from the notice. For dimensions, keep the same width-to-height ratio as the cm values. For example, 4 cm × 2 cm has the same ratio as 400 × 200 px.

Is this an official SSC website?

No. FormFit Image is an independent free tool and has no connection to the Staff Selection Commission.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Processing happens in your browser and the file never leaves your device.
