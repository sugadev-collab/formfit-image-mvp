How to compress a photo to 50 KB

  1. The Under 50 KB preset is selected. If your form says 20–50 KB, tap the Photo 20–50 KB preset instead, so the file isn't too small.
  2. If the form asks for exact pixels, enter the width and height. Otherwise leave them empty and the tool picks a sharp size that fits.
  3. Choose your image, tap Resize & Compress, check that every item shows ✅, then download.

50 KB is one of the most common upper limits for passport-style photos on recruitment, exam, scholarship and admission portals. Many portals also have a minimum, often 20 KB, and reject files that are too small. That's why this tool lets you set both limits and checks the result against them.

Always check the size rules in the official notification for your form.

Frequently asked questions

My form says 20 KB to 50 KB. How do I get a file in that range?

Tap the Photo 20–50 KB preset, or enter 20 as Min KB and 50 as Max KB. The result shows a pass or fail check for the range.

What pixel size should a 50 KB photo be?

Use the dimensions given in your form's notice. If none are given, leave the fields empty and the tool chooses a sharp size that fits under 50 KB.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Processing happens in your browser and the file never leaves your device.
